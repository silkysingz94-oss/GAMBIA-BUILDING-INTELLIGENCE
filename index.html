10 | DATA & METHODOLOGY

Building counts are the number of valid polygon features in the loaded dataset after spatial filtering.

Footprint area is calculated from polygon geometry using Turf.js geodesic area.

Building density is the count of footprint features divided by selected-area square kilometres.

This dashboard does not infer occupancy, number of households, floors, building use, construction date or population.

The original Earth Engine script assigns a year label to one collection. It does not provide verified annual building snapshots.

Google Open Buildings is a building-footprint dataset; verify the exact source asset, coverage and update date before interpreting the results as national or historical statistics.
